The Problem

Belonging is the one human need the social stack stopped serving.

Nearly 1 in 4 people worldwide feel lonely, and in 2023 the WHO declared loneliness a pressing global health threat. Yet the products built to "connect" us optimize for attention or for matching — never for a small group that knows your name and expects you back next week.

What today's social stack delivers

  • Reddit / X: content + outrage
  • Discord: chat for gamers + work
  • Tinder / Bumble: a match, then churn
  • Meetup: dormant IRL stack

What no one delivers

A persistent small group of 8–40 people who share a specific interest, recognise each other, and show up again — the thing every human had before the internet, now open to anyone, in any language.

Source: WHO Commission on Social Connection, 2023; Meta–Gallup State of Social Connections, 2023.

The Vision

A place to belong — small, alive, and without a language barrier.

Online Tribes is building the missing primitive: small interest-based tribes (8–40 people) with the tools to stay alive — and because everything a member creates is translated in real time, a tribe can span the world. Belonging without borders.

Small by design

Tribes cap and split as they grow. Belonging breaks above ~40 members; we honour that.

Leader-led, not feed-led

Owners set rules, run rituals, and earn from engagement. Incentives aligned with retention, not impressions.

Borderless by default

Real-time translation on every post, comment, and message. A member in Warsaw and one in São Paulo share one room — each reading in their own language.

Language is the largest, oldest barrier to belonging online. We remove it automatically — so who you connect with is decided by what you love, never by what you speak.

Self-Improving Product

A product that reads its own data, helps build itself, and plans what's next.

We wired MCP servers across the entire stack. The same system that runs the app now reads its own analytics, assists development, and drafts the roadmap — so two founders ship like a much larger team, and the build-measure-learn loop gets faster every week.

Reads its own analytics

MCP pulls BigQuery & GA4 directly — funnels, cohorts, per-feature usage — on demand. 193 events, 84+ pre-built queries.

Helps build & test

Agentic development plus on-device mobile testing, so changes are shipped and verified with far less human time.

Helps plan

The system surfaces the leak, proposes the fix, and feeds the sprint board — turning data into prioritized work automatically.

Keeps rooms alive

Rotating AI personas detect thin or quiet tribes and intervene in real time to maintain interaction density.

The instrumentation isn't a dashboard — it's the engine. An autonomous loop that compounds: every week of data makes the next week's product decisions sharper.

What's Next

From rooms to rituals: the activity-first tribe.

Chat keeps a tribe talking; recurring shared activity keeps it alive. Our next chapter makes the tribe revolve around things members do together — with AI forming small pods and facilitating them, so strangers always have something to do and a reason to come back.

Live "doings"

Schedulable synchronous activities inside a tribe: co-work sessions, discussion circles, skill practice, watch-alongs, challenges.

AI pods

The matching engine groups ~5 members into small activity pods inside a tribe — connection by design, not a dead match card.

AI facilitation

Icebreakers, prompts, and light roles so a pod of strangers always has something to say — the structure most groups lack.

The repeat loop

After an activity, nudge the same pod to do it again. Repetition is the retention engine — momentum from showing up.

Offline bridge

Once trust forms online, surface an optional real-world meetup for the pod — the belonging payoff.

More languages

Expand real-time translation to every major user-base language — widening the borderless tribe even further.

Every item above is already specced on our board. This is execution, not exploration.

Market

Belonging is the only social vertical where retention compounds with depth.

Attention (Meta, X, TikTok)

$700B+ market. Feed-driven; depth degrades engagement. Saturated.

Matching (Tinder, Bumble)

$8B+ market. Retention collapses post-match by design. Margins under pressure.

Belonging (open)

The only social primitive where deeper relationships compound usage. No category leader.

Comparables — proof the category is fundable

  • Discord — $15B valuation; group-chat for synchronous use (gaming, work).
  • Geneva — raised $26M; group-chat without the tribe-leader incentive layer.
  • Mighty Networks — $100M+ raised; creator-led communities, not interest-based small groups.
  • Skool — profitable; courses-led community, not horizontal belonging.

None build the small-group, horizontal belonging primitive for individuals — and none make it borderless with real-time translation. That's the category we're opening.

Business Model

The rails are built. We turn them on with traction.

Monetization is engineered and live in the app — three revenue rails, ready to scale. We've deliberately kept the throttle low, because for belonging, retention precedes revenue: a community you don't leave is one you'll happily pay for.

B2C Premium — live

Stripe in the app. Unlimited joins, ad-free, premium matching and translation.

Ads — live

AdMob in the app. The free tier monetizes itself as volume scales.

Tribe-owner take-rate — next

Owners earn from engaged members; platform fee 15–20%. Turns best users into aligned, paid distribution.

Three revenue rails already in the product. The question isn't "can we build monetization" — it's "how big does belonging get."
