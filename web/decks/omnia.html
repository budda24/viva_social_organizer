The Problem

Belonging is the one human need the social stack stopped serving.

61% of adults under 25 report serious loneliness, and the U.S. Surgeon General declared it a public-health emergency in 2023. Yet the products built to "connect" us optimize for attention or for matching — never for a small group that knows your name and expects you back next week.

What today's social stack delivers

  • Reddit / X: content + outrage
  • Discord: chat for gamers + work
  • Tinder / Bumble: a match, then churn
  • Meetup: dormant IRL stack

What it doesn't deliver

A persistent small group of 8–40 people who share a specific interest, recognise each other, and show up again. The thing every human had before the internet.

Source: Cigna Loneliness Index 2024; U.S. Surgeon General Advisory, 2023.

The Solution

The tribe is the unit. Not the feed.

Online Tribes lets anyone create or join a small interest-based tribe (8–40 people) and gives the tribe the tools to stay alive: shared posts, group chat, AI-assisted onboarding, and a paid leadership path for the owners who hold a community together.

Small by design

Tribes cap and split as they grow. Belonging breaks above ~40 members; we honour that.

Leader-led, not feed-led

Owners set rules, run rituals, and earn from engagement. Incentive aligned with retention, not impressions.

AI as introducer

AI matches users to tribes by emotional context, not keywords. Not an AI friend — an AI that finds you a real one.

Broad, cheap install buying (and organic spillover) brought us volume from markets we don't serve — and our ICP arrives into tribes with almost no one like them in the room. Belonging needs density. We never built ICP density, so US users find no one to belong to and churn on day one. That's a cold-start problem, not proof the ICP doesn't want this.

Two instrumentation gaps we're fixing first (both P0)

Tribe-join is half-dark

141 users viewed a tribe in 30d, but tribe_join_completed fired for only 6 — the event is under-instrumented across join paths. We can't yet see our most important action clearly.

AI introducer opens, then silence

165 users opened the AI introducer in 30d; 0 downstream messages are recorded. Either users bounce or the event isn't wired. We will know within two weeks.

Market

Belonging is the only social vertical where retention compounds with depth.

Attention (Meta, X, TikTok)

$700B+ market. Feed-driven; depth degrades engagement. Saturated.

Matching (Tinder, Bumble)

$8B+ market. Retention collapses post-match by design. Margins under pressure.

Belonging (open)

The only social primitive where deeper relationships compound usage. No category leader.

Comparables — proof the category is fundable

  • Discord — $15B valuation; group-chat for synchronous use (gaming, work).
  • Geneva — raised $26M; group-chat without the tribe-leader incentive layer.
  • Mighty Networks — $100M+ raised; creator-led communities, not interest-based small groups.
  • Skool — profitable; courses-led community, not horizontal belonging.

None build the small-group, horizontal belonging primitive for individuals. That's the opening — if someone can crack ICP density.

Business Model

Monetization is a hypothesis, not a result — and we'll say so.

The rails are built and live in code: a consumer Premium subscription and a tribe-owner take-rate. To date that's produced just 3 purchases. We are not pitching revenue. We are pitching retention — revenue is the next question, not this one.

B2C — Premium (live in code)

  • Unlimited joins, ad-free, premium matching
  • Pricing TBD post-ICP (Western pricing power, not global)
  • Conversion instrumentation being wired this sprint

Tribe-owner economics (next)

  • Owners earn from engaged members; platform fee 15–20%
  • Turns best users into aligned, paid distribution
  • Activates only once ICP retention is real
Why we lead with retention, not revenue

For belonging, retention precedes monetization: a community you don't leave is one you'll eventually pay for. Selling subscriptions into 3.85% retention would just buy churn. Fix the room first.
